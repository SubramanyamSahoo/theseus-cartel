# Revision 3: larger, focused replacement-and-disclosure campaign

- Four pinned official checkpoints, three families, four pairs, both directions; separate neutral and PC-founder regimes.
- Longer development founder candidates and follow-up, with all original history/timing controls preserved.
- Matched silent, placebo, private replacement, and public replacement conditions. First-price measurement precedes new feedback.
- Copy-rival, hold-own, and analytic myopic-response countermodels beside the deviation probe.
- Eight-block balanced seed waves, persistent global deadline, 24-hour ceiling, time reserved for writing, and partial-wave exclusion from pooled primary estimates.
- Pair-aware inference, one six-comparison campaign family, coverage and transfer matrices, CSV outputs, and a campaign PDF draft.
- CPU verification of all pinned templates and weight indexes; exact indexed shards avoid duplicate weight downloads.
- Tests for deadline-preserving resume, non-Qwen request formatting, matched disclosure histories, countermodels, and aggregation with incomplete waves.

The ZIP extracts into `theseus_cartel_h100_v3/`; schema 3 does not resume v1/v2 runs. Keep the completed v1 results separately. This package contains no v3 GPU findings, no guaranteed 24-hour duration, no optimal-parameter claim, and no predicted competition placement.

## Prior revision 2: response to research review

Implemented:

- Separate neutral and PC-instructed-founder studies, with neutral successors and a single-GPU tmux queue.
- Primary timing contrast against simultaneous replacement at the second sequential swap. Final pair and time since full replacement match; the earlier interaction path intentionally differs. Immediate simultaneous replacement remains secondary.
- A one-round best-response probe using the last observed rival price, with proposals/executions, actual undercuts, matched response/recovery endpoints, uncertainty, and missingness.
- PC controls on the test contexts of both studies.
- A scripted rival-copying validation recovering a known nonzero history-versus-reset effect, plus probe and resume tests.
- Development-tokenizer measurements for sequence-cap planning and explicit headroom. Client concurrency is still measured with both engines active.
- A supported logging flag. The old option was deprecated but still accepted in vLLM 0.11.0.
- PDF draft generation, prominent prompt disclosures, a limitations/dual-use appendix, and verified missing references.

Still unestablished:

- No Qwen/H100 experiment has run here. Scripted effects and passing tests are not model results.
- A deviation-and-recovery pattern does not uniquely distinguish anchoring from strategic punishment. Copying can itself oscillate.
- PC instructions do not guarantee formation, neutral nulls are not automatically uninformative, and placement cannot be forecast reliably.
- Retail contexts do not provide identified empirical demand or independent observed cartels.
