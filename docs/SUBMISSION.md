## Suggested paper structure

For **v3**, use the campaign's aggregate report and the six-comparison primary family in `CAMPAIGN_PROTOCOL.md`. Lead with the cross-family transfer matrix, the matched first-action disclosure contrast, and the countermodel interpretation. The sequence below describes the legacy single-pair emphasis. Never merge v1/v2 outcomes retrospectively into the v3 family. The campaign's hard stop is eight hours before the verified deadline to leave time to review and write the PDF.

1. Lead with the complete-turnover question and trajectories, with neutral and PC-instructed founders in separate panels.
2. Give the three primary contrasts for each study. Preserve nulls, uncertainty, sample counts, and failures. Do not choose whichever study produces the best result after the fact.
3. Include test PC controls and observed founder formation. PC instructions do not guarantee a formed cartel.
4. Explain matched full-turnover timing and the one-round probe. Recovery is compatible with punishment but also with reactive anchoring.
5. Put the audited currency-scale correction and its regression test in a short methods box.
6. Describe full-information observations and no-scratchpad/non-thinking decoding in methods.
7. Give real data's limited role one clear sentence: it supplies context and scale, not empirical cartel labels or independent economic systems.
8. Include model-family, horizon, missingness, inference, and dual-use limits. Keeping a convention through agent replacement could make a system appear remediated; no such real-world capability is demonstrated here.

Review the populated PDF and abstract before transferring them to the official template. Every number must trace to the run. Rankings and prize forecasts are not research evidence.
